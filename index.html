04World models: learning dynamics from pixels

TOY EXAMPLE · OPEN SOURCE

Latent world model for Pendulum-v1

A small JEPA-style world model inspired by LeWorldModel learns from 64×64 images and torque, predicting the next latent state with a SIGReg regularizer. Physical state is never used as a training target; probes check what the latent space recovered.

Moving from a single frame to a five-frame history raised the angular-velocity probe R² from −1.45 to 0.997 in exploratory runs. The clip shows original frames (left) and decoder reconstructions (right).

02 / RISK AND UNCERTAINTY

Risk-aware and risk-budgeted control

Probabilistic sensing and CVaR extend safety filtering to uncertain environments. Risk-budgeted intervention adapts controller conservatism as safety conditions change.

Multi-modal sensing, full-scale vehicle evaluation, and 1,500 Monte Carlo trials of risk-budgeted control.

03 / EVALUATION AND CERTIFICATION

Trajectory safety evaluation and certification

Barrier-based evaluation extends to trajectories produced by an independent planner, combining risk-aware margins with conformal calibration.
